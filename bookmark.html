Bookmark


The goal of this project was to design, print, and finish a custom bookmark for Eshleman Elementary School. We had to decide on content and make five thumbnail sketches. Then we chose our best sketch make made a rough layout of that one, indicating point size, leading, and fonts used in the design. We looked at our classmates design and gave feedback and finalized our designs. We then exported those files and made a plate layout. We made a plate and then made 75 sheets from the plate. We let the prints dry and then cut them down the next day. I ended up actually printing the design on the top but the other one was my other design that I was considering making. This is an adorable project because we are learning how to design and print, but we are also making bookmarks for little kids and actually contributing to a school library.
