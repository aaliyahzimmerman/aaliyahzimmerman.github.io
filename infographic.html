Infographic

The software we used for this prject was Adobe Illustrator. We have used this a few times before, so I was a little familiar with it, and trie to make the best looking infographic as possible. The purpose for this prject was to communicate a technology using a variety of resources, and to determine the origin, principle, uses, impacts, and possible future of the selected communication technology and to synthesize that information into a visual presentation. For my topic I chose social media, a very current form of communication in today's society. My infographic explains the uses and such of social media, as well as how it can be dangerous. For the infographic, we had to do some research and include as many facts as needed to explain how our communication system works. There are a lot of ways that social media impacts the world and they are all explained on my infographic.
